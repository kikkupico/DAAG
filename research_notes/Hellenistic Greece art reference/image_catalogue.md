# Image catalogue: public-domain / CC0 images for Hellenistic Greece (323-31 BC)

## How this was verified

- Commons: every file below was looked up through the Commons API (`prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata`). The **Licence** column is the `LicenseShortName` that the API returned on 2026-10-01. "Public domain" is the label Commons applies (age or PD-author tags). Commons labels are the uploader's tagging and are not a legal opinion, so check the file page before commercial use.
- **Direct image URL** is an `upload.wikimedia.org/.../thumb/...` link at 960 px wide (the standard thumbnail step nearest the requested ~1000 px; Commons no longer serves arbitrary widths). Small originals link to the original. Every one of these URLs was fetched with an HTTP range request and returned an `image/*` content type (see the verification log at the end). Nothing was kept.
- Met Open Access: objects were fetched from `collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/{id}`. All rows have `isPublicDomain = true`; the image link is `primaryImageSmall` (the full-size `primaryImage` field is also in the API). Note that the old `/v1/search` endpoint was retired on 2026-10-01; use `/v1.1/search`.
- Date column: Commons rows show the uploader's date field, which for photographs is the date of the photo and not of the object. The object date is in the notes column where it matters.
- Flag key: **[Roman copy]**, **[later recon]** (modern or post-antique reconstruction or drawing), **[not Hellenistic]**, **[unverified]**.
- Order within each table: public domain or CC0 first. CC BY / CC BY-SA items are in a separate labelled fallback table and need attribution (and, for BY-SA, share-alike).

## Cross-cutting gaps (things I looked for and did not find as PD/CC0)

- **Bohn's plates of the Great Altar of Zeus itself** (the 1896 Pergamon reconstruction): not found as a Commons file. Searches for "Pergamon Altar Bohn", "AvP III.2" and the Pergamon Altar categories returned only Bohn's AvP II and AvP IV plates (Athena sanctuary, Dionysos temple, theatre terrace; all listed above) and Humann's sketch. The altar's `Category:Pergamon Altar plan` holds PD plans of the museum installation (`Grundriss-Aufstellung-Pergamonaltar.jpg`, `Altaar van Pergamon Plattegrond, GD004669.jpg`; not downloaded or checked beyond the search-API licence label "Public domain"). The Berlin museum photographs of the altar are all CC BY / BY-SA.
- **Priene city plan from the Wiegand/Schrader 1904 volume**: not found as a standalone PD plate. Only the Bouleuterion state plan (from the same book) and the Wellcome scan of a Priene plan (CC BY 4.0) were found.
- **Rhodes city plan**: nothing usable found on Commons by search. Rhodes is covered only by the CC0 Heemskerck Colossus engraving (1572, imaginative) and coins.
- **Artillery drawings (Schramm 1918, Marsden-based)**: no Commons file found. The Gastraphetes manuscript copy and the Saalburg reconstruction photo are the nearest PD items.
- **Hellenistic ship reliefs / Ptolemaic ship depictions** as PD/CC0 images: none found by search. Nearest: Demetrios Poliorketes tetradrachm (Nike on a ship's prow, Met), the Palestrina Nile mosaic (boats), the Cypriot boat relief (Met), the Nike of Samothrace (its ship-prow base is not in the CC0 photos listed), and Witsen's print of the Syracusia (post-antique).
- **Decrees on stelai**: no good Hellenistic decree-stele photo identified as PD/CC0 within the search budget. The Cleveland Pamphylian stele (inscription plus relief) and the Heraklion gravestone are funerary, not decrees.
- **Greek helmets of the Hellenistic period (Greek, not Italic)**: the Met items found are Etruscan/Italic. A Reggio Calabria Phrygian-type helmet with cuirass and greaves (BY-SA 4.0) is the best Greek-world fallback.
- **Hellenistic pack-animal / cart reliefs**: the Met donkeys found are Cypriot Archaic (600-480 BC) and are not listed. The only Hellenistic-context donkey is the Rhodes museum figurine, whose date is unverified.
- **Boxer at Rest, Alexander Sarcophagus, Pergamon Altar, Lindos, Kyrenia**: no PD/CC0 photo found; only BY-SA / BY fallbacks are given.
- Licence statuses for physical works (e.g. museum-object photographs tagged "Public domain") rest on the Commons tag; I did not independently audit the photographer or the underlying 2D-reproduction argument.
