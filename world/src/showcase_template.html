Arche and Paxos

The two islands of the series, modelled in full: every harbour, house, hall and library, the mountain and its siege, the bay and its two assemblies, and the scholars’ coast.

Each island is built from the setting’s own descriptions: Arche’s three trading houses round its mountain, the camps in the gullies and the ruined wall on the summit; Paxos’s three sections on the ground of Leros, with the Chamber and the Tholos facing each other from their summits across the bay, the oracle on the southern neck, and the libraries of the scholars’ coast.

Everything is in place at once. The renders below are taken from that one model of each island, in the light of different hours and seasons. No people are shown: the islands are lived in through their objects.

ΑΡΧΗ

Arche, the island of foundations

One massif fills the island. Three houses, known by number, sit a third of the way round the coast from one another, joined by a single ring road; on the mountain, four mercenaries camp in the gullies and four bandits hold the crown.

Two cities, two ways of keeping one law scroll at the top of the island, and below the southern neck a coast of libraries that keep many copies of one compendium and hold no assembly.

Made with

Built by script. Terrain, buildings, columns and roofs tile by tile, ships, carts, pottery, the olive, cypress, pine and oak trees, and the painted panel in the stoa are all generated.

Materials, rocks, shrubs and skies are from Poly Haven (CC0).

The statues on the Chamber’s terrace are scans of museum pieces from Three D Scans, published free of copyright restrictions.

The ground of Paxos is Leros: its coastline from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), its heights from the AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM and other open data).
