One problem, three settings

Many machines, acting as one

Every book in the series asks how a group of machines, each with its own memory and joined only by messages, can behave as a single trustworthy one. These three books take the question in order.

Arche finds an order of events that machines can agree on without a shared clock. Paxos keeps one log of commands in such an order while machines stop, restart and lose messages. Schedia keeps the same log with one machine in charge at a time, and shows how another takes over when it falls silent without losing anything the last one promised.
