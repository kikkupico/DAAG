A Round of Your Own

Set places round a ring, decide how long their messengers may take and what the road may do to them, who may fall silent and who may lie, and what the places want together. The round acts your world out, and beside it every idea from the books that answers it, every wall that stands in the way, and the book to read next.

Pick the number of processes, the timing and channel model, the fault model and the guarantees you want. The round simulates the model, and the panel gives every result from the papers that applies: achievable bounds, impossibility results, the techniques at work, and the paper that covers it.

Each box on the road is a place; each dot crossing the round is a messenger. A cross is a place fallen silent, a red ring one that may lie; the shaded loop is a cut. The bands outside show two groups and, in gold, who they share. Press a place to change it. Each box is a process; each dot a message in flight. A cross marks a crash, a red ring a Byzantine process; the hatched loop is a partition. Outer bands show two quorums, their intersection in gold. Click a process to cycle its fault.
