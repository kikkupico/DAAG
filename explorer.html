Navigation Controls

  • Orbit Mode: Left Drag to Rotate, Right Drag to Pan, Scroll to Zoom.
  • Fly Mode: Mouse Drag to Look, WASD to fly, Q/E for altitude, Shift for boost.
  • Focal Length: Select below or drag the slider.
  • Screenshot: Click "Copy Screenshot" or press P.
  • Cast: Turn on Drop, then click the island to place the chosen character in the chosen pose; drag before letting go to face them. Click a character to select or move it; Delete removes it, [ ] turn it.
  • Set: Pick an interior set (the Chamber) in the side panel; it replaces the island's own building, and Flycam takes you inside.
  • Scene: Click "Copy Scene" or press C to copy a previs shot (camera and cast).
